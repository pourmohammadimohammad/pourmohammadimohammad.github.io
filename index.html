Mo Pourmohammadi

PhD Candidate in Finance  ·  Yale University

My research lies at the intersection of empirical and theoretical asset pricing, where I use methods from machine learning and financial econometrics.

I am a PhD student in Finance at Yale University. Before joining Yale, I completed an MSc in Finance at Bocconi University and a BSc in Applied Mathematics at Sharif University of Technology.

I have a deep appreciation for mathematics and problem-solving, reflected in earning a Silver Medal in the Iranian National Mathematics Olympiad.
